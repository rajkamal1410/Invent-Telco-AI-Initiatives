Sovereign AI & operator strategies

Source: Fierce Network, NVIDIA, AWS, Microsoft
Operators pursue sovereign AI to control data and latency — building capability stacks (model hosting, inference at the edge) while partnering with cloud and GPU vendors. Bell’s approach: build the orchestration shell, partner for chips — a pattern other operators may replicate to retain control and manage vendor lock-in.

5G evolution & standards

Sources: 3GPP, ITU, Ericsson Mobility Report
5G continues to expand across three vectors: eMBB, mMTC and URLLC. Workstreams progress on 5G-Advanced and early 6G research; standardisation remains coordinated through 3GPP and ITU. Operators focus on slicing, private networks and enterprise vertical integrations.
See 3GPP • ITU • Ericsson Mobility Report

Network cloudification & interoperability

Open RAN and cloud-native core deployments continue to mature; vendor interoperability tests and OSS/BSS portability are the current friction points. Expect more vendor partnerships and neutral-host models in dense markets.
Sources: TelecomTV, LightReading, RCR Wireless

Regulation & market structure

EU regulatory moves (e.g., social media rules for children) and national data-centre planning shifts (e.g., Scotland) influence telco service strategies and infrastructure planning — compliance will shape product rollouts and partnership models.
Sources: Mobile World Live, Capacity
