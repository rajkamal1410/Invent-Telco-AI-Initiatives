Unlocking value from Rakuten Mobile data

Rakuten Mobile is sitting on a large, high-value data estate and is seeking a clear path to monetize it responsibly. The strategy is to turn internal and external data assets into data products, sharing models, and revenue-generating services while building a governance foundation that enables trust, interoperability, and scalable commercialization.

Marketplace

Data Marketplace as the commercialization backbone

Why it matters

The Data Marketplace connects producers and consumers of data products to enable rapid sharing and monetization in a frictionless model. It complements lakehouse architectures and supports emerging patterns such as Data Mesh and Data Fabric.

Producer and consumer value

  • Producers can register, publish, and govern data products.
  • Consumers get a single shop front for requesting access and provisioning.
  • Business units gain faster time to insight and stronger data democratization.
Data producers Create, register, and deliver data products with governance and visibility on consumption.
Data consumers Discover, request, and provision access through a shopping-cart like experience.
Organization Enable scale, trust, privacy alignment, and data monetization across business ecosystems.
