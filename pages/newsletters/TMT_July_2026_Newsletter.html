Highlights — Quick summary

  • Bookings (YTD) at 3,880 KE; Jun month performance tracked at 47% toward the YTD target (4150 KE by end-Jun).
  • Pipeline TCV = 5,212 KE; 73% of Pipeline (≈3,803 KE) is in Stages 6 & 7 — priority for closure.
  • Pipeline-to-Sold coverage compressed to ~1.34x (prior 2.25x → 1.36x in slides). Recovering to ≥2.0x is immediate focus.
  • ARVE ≈ 73% (as of Jun). INTER CM (Contribution Margin) ≈ 53% (target 46%).
  • People & resourcing: 47 resources on projects, Bench ≈ 10, 3 new joiners confirmed, 4 hires planned.

Key takeaways & recommended actions

  • Accelerate closure efforts for INTER pipeline opportunities in Stages 6 & 7 — these comprise the majority of near-term booking potential.
  • Leverage bench resources for RFP activities: align bench to bid codes and prioritize rapid deployment where it helps win deals.
  • Account owners for INTER accounts: explore new roles within existing engagements and prioritize conversion-focused actions.
  • Plan rapid deployment of three new lateral hires joining in Aug 2026 to active opportunities for quick ramp-up.
  • Drive AI skills adoption through self-paced learning, internal “AI for Us, From Us” forums, and assigned trainings so resources are ready for upcoming AI-driven opportunities.
