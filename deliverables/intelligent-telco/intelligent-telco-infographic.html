Primary monetization levers

Where value is created

📡

Network APIs

Turn internal network capabilities into programmable services through GSMA Open Gateway and ecosystem APIs for authentication, trust, quality, and edge access.

🔐

Identity & fraud intelligence

Use number verification, SIM swap, KYC support, and device trust to reduce fraud while creating premium identity services for financial and digital businesses.

📍

Mobility & enterprise intelligence

Package aggregated movement and network insights into retail, planning, logistics, transport, and public sector decision support products.

🤖

AI-enabled ecosystem value

Combine telco data with broader digital experiences, commerce, payments, and AI agents to create ecosystem-led customer engagement.

Operating model

Simple, scalable, and governable

Four-step path to scale

1
Prove value internally

Start with network, customer, and operational use cases that improve revenue, cost, and experience.

2
Build trust into architecture

Embed privacy, governance, consent, data quality, explainability, and human accountability from day one.

3
Productize differentiated signals

Focus on network-originated capabilities that competitors cannot easily replicate.

4
Scale through ecosystems

Use partnerships and standardized interfaces to distribute capabilities across broader developer and enterprise communities.

Essential governance principles

A
Trust

Protect customer data through clear purpose, consent, and access controls.

B
Explainability

Ensure decisions can be traced, audited, and reviewed by human owners.

C
Cybersecurity

Secure API exposure and manage risk as the operator expands external interfaces.

D
Human accountability

AI can augment decisions, but trust and supervision must remain with accountable operators.

Bottom line

The strategic opportunity

Network as platform

Operators can convert network capabilities into reusable, programmable business products rather than static utility services.

Trust as advantage

Identity, verification, and fraud signals become premium assets when they are governed, privacy-safe, and commercially clear.

AI as enabler

AI agents can orchestrate services across ecosystems, but only within explainable, auditable, and governed workflows.
